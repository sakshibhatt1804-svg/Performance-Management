2 · Root-cause diagnosis

Five causes: opaque, rater-dependent, one-size-fits-all

We separated facts (filings) from perceptions (anonymous reviews). A review theme counts only when two sources agree.

Medium

Appraisal mechanics are opaque

"No visibility into mechanics of how appraisals are done" (Glassdoor, 2026). The annual report discloses no appraisal process, which clashes with Groww's value of complete transparency.

Medium

Outcomes depend on who rates you

Favouritism and politics recur across AmbitionBox and Glassdoor. Research finds most rating variance reflects the rater, not the ratee.

Medium

One system for very different jobs

Pods, high-volume support, advisory sales and compliance share one template. Support has 50% attrition and its reviews single out "appraisal and hike".

Medium-high

Pay-for-performance feels asymmetric

FY26 median pay +18.1% and non-managerial +11.7%, against +66.7% for senior management. Reviews mention "bad hikes".

Inferred

Scale, M&A and regulation add risk

Headcount +27% in nine months including Fisdom. Growth in MTF, lending and wealth means sales-only targets can reward mis-selling.

How we read reviews

Most review pages hold only 1–3 reviews and are anonymous, so we used them as themes, never as facts. We excluded look-alike entities and also recorded the positives: learning, a flat culture, approachable founders.

4 · Redesigned end-to-end process

One cycle, nine steps, a human decision at every gate

PlanQuarterly OKRs; goal coach checks SMART + OKR link
Check inEvery two weeks; notes feed the evidence ledger
EvidenceMetrics, kudos, QA, conduct flow in continuously
ReviewManager sees the AI band and its explanation
AuditFairness monitor flags leniency, gaps, recency
CalibratePanel reviews alerts; overrides justified
ReleaseEmployee sees the rating, evidence and math
ContestIndependent HRBP decides within 10 working days
Grow & rewardDevelopment plan; merit band; conduct malus

Pink outline = AI-assisted step. Dark = human decision. Governance: an AI and Model Risk Committee (HR Analytics, Compliance, Legal/DPO, employee representatives) owns weights, thresholds and the kill switch, and runs a quarterly bias audit.

5 · AI and data architecture with safeguards

Explainable services behind a privacy gateway

Gender and location never reach the scorer; only the fairness audit reads them. The LLM summarises and rewrites, always citing source records, and never scores anyone.

Explainable and abstaining

Factor contributions add up exactly to the score. No band is suggested with under 4 evidence items or under 3 months of tenure.

Contestable and overseen

Overrides need evidence-based, bias-free reasons. Employees can appeal, and HR holds a kill switch.

Private and auditable

The design follows the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025, with no keystroke or chat surveillance, and a SHA-256 audit chain. It treats the EU AI Act's high-risk rules as its benchmark.

8 · Implementation roadmap and measurable impact

Pilot where the pain is highest, then scale with proof

Pilot

Customer Success plus two Platforms pods. Manager training and a DPDP notice to employees. Shadow mode: flags go to HR only.

3–6 months

Learn

Employee-facing explanations and appeals. First bias audit. Tune weights with employee representatives. Connect the LLM summariser.

6–12 months

Scale

All four role families including Fisdom and Wealth. Pay and ESOP decisions use calibrated ratings. External audit before FY28.
